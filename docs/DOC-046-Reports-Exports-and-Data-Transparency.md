# DOC-046 — Reports, Exports and Data Transparency

## Unified reports

- Portfolio
- Scanner
- Alert history
- Backtest
- Experiment matrix
- Account/security export
- Admin operational reports

Her rapor generatedAt, dataCutoffAt, methodology versions, source revisions, warnings, partial/stale status, owner ve expiry taşır.

## Güvenlik

- ownership/IDOR
- short-lived links
- formula injection
- size/rate limits
- audit
- expiry/cleanup
- no secret/internal payload

## Transparency

Indicator, valuation, risk, backtest, benchmark, adjustment, freshness ve notEvaluable reason görünür olmalıdır.
