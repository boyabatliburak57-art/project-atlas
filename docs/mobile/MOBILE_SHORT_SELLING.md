# Mobile Short-Selling Research

The mobile surface keeps two concepts separate:

1. A short-selling or margin-trading restriction is a canonical `MarketMeasure`.
2. Short-selling activity is an observed time-series record with trade date, quantity, value, and turnover share only where supplied.

When activity capability is provider/license gated, the screen shows that state and does not render zero-valued rows. Delayed data is labelled delayed. No “pressure,” bullish/bearish, buy/sell, or price-impact score is produced.

The methodology entry explains publication versus effective dates and states that the information is not investment advice. Export/share actions are absent when canonical redistribution policy prohibits them.
