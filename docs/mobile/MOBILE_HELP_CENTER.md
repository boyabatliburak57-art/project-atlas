# Mobile Help and Methodology Center

Help content is organized by the versioned content registry: Getting Started, Market Data, Scanner, Watchlists and Alerts, Portfolio, Strategy Lab, Reports, Account, Notifications, Data and Methodology, and Privacy and Security. Articles carry locale, version and updated time; related content is linked from feature screens.

Search is NFKC-normalized, bounded, debounced and cancellable. Empty/no-result/offline-cached states are distinct. Raw help queries are not telemetry payloads.

The Methodology Center is the common destination for market freshness, scanner, indicators, patterns, cost basis, performance, risk, backtesting, benchmarking, corporate actions and data quality. Each item exposes version, effective time, assumptions, limitations, source and related features. Legal content is technically integrated but remains `LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED` and `NOT_FOR_PRODUCTION_PUBLICATION`.
