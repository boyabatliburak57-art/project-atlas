# Institutional Net Money Flow

Net Institutional Flow is the sum of covered institutional buy values minus covered institutional sell values for the selected actual trading sessions. It is not literal capital entering or leaving the exchange and does not predict price direction.

The methodology identifier is `institutional-net-flow-v1`. Results expose covered observation/institution counts and quality. Missing sides make a row not evaluable; they are never converted to zero. Top-1/3/5 concentration is a descriptive share of covered absolute values and carries its own methodology version.
