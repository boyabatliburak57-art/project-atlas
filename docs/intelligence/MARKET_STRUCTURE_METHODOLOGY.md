# Market Structure Methodology

An exchange measure is shown according to its published evidence and effective interval. `publishedAt` is when the source published it, `availableAt` is when Atlas could lawfully know it, and the effective interval determines scheduled/active/expired state. A later announcement does not become active early.

A short-selling restriction describes a rule. Short-selling activity describes provider-supplied observed quantity/value/share and is not evidence of direction, causality, or an investment signal. Delayed data is labeled delayed; unavailable licensed data is provider/license gated. Atlas makes no claim that a measure or its removal predicts price movement.
