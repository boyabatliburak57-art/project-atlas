# Institutional Intelligence

Atlas institutional research follows **one institutional data model, multiple research surfaces**. Markets → Institutional, company summaries, canonical institution profiles, future Radar conditions, Compare metrics, and anomaly inputs query `InstitutionDomain`, `InstitutionalFlowDomain`, and `SettlementDomain`; none owns a copied leaderboard or trend table.

AKD answers who traded on a `tradeDate`. Takas answers where holdings were recorded on a `settlementDate`. Responses retain `availableAt`, provider capability, runtime health, quality, coverage, delivery delay, provenance, and license restrictions. These observations are descriptive research data and never produce AL/SAT or bullish/bearish advice.

Production remains fail-closed when a licensed adapter is unavailable. Test fixtures are selected only by the local E2E compile boundary and are replaced with empty modules in production bundles.
